## Life

مالك بن أنس — born in Madinah in **93 AH** (711 CE). *Imam of the House* and founder of the Maliki school. He taught in the Prophet's Mosque for over forty years.

## The Muwatta

His **Muwatta** is the earliest surviving hadith collection. See [[book:HDT-muwattaMalik]].

- Compiled over forty years
- Around 1720 narrations
