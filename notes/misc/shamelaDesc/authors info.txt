This is a screenshot of the popular Arabic Islamic digital library desktop software, **Al-Maktaba Al-Shamela (المكتبة الشاملة)**, displaying a biographical entry for the pre-Islamic Arabic poet **Amr ibn Qami'ah (عمرو بن قميئة)**.

---

### Key Sections of the Interface:

#### 1. **Main Content Window (Center Left)**
Displays the biography and historical details of **Amr ibn Qami'ah**:
* **Name & Dates:** 
  * `عمرو بن قميئة (540 م)`
  * `عَمْرو بن قَمِيئَة (نحو 180 - 85 ق هـ = نحو 448 - 540 م)` (approx. 180–85 BH / 448–540 AD)
* **Full Lineage:** 
  * `عمرو بن قميئة بن ذريح بن سعد بن مالك الثعلبي البكري الوائلي النزاري`
* **Biography Points:**
  * **Prominent pre-Islamic poet:** (`شاعر جاهلي مقدم.`)
  * **Life summary:** He grew up as an orphan, lived in Al-Hira for a time, accompanied Hujr (the father of the poet Imru' al-Qais), and later traveled with Imru' al-Qais on his journey to Caesar. He died along the way, earning him the nickname **"Al-Da'i'" (The Lost One)** (`فكان يقال له «الضائع»`).
  * **Style:** Known for his broad imagination in poetry (`وكان واسع الخيال في شعره.`).
  * **Literary Mention:** Imru' al-Qais famously lamented his death in verse (`وفيه يقول امرؤ القيس: «بكى صاحبي لما رأى الدر دونه...»`).
  * **Works:** Possesses a published Diwan (poetry collection).
* **Footnote / Citations (Bottom of text):** Lists classic reference works containing his biography, including *Kitab al-Aghani* (v. 16, p. 158), *Al-Amidi*, *Al-Shi'r wa al-Shu'ara*, *Al-Lubab*, and others.

---

#### 2. **Right Sidebar (Author List & Index)**
* **Header:** Displays `بيانات المؤلفين والكتب` (Authors and Books Data) with a total of **3,188 authors** (`عدد المؤلفين: 3188`).
* **Author Table:** Chronologically lists classic poets/authors by their death year:
  1. **Amr ibn Qami'ah** (540 AD)
  2. **Imru' al-Qais** (545 AD)
  3. **Al-Afwah al-Awdi** (560 AD)
  4. **Tarafa ibn al-Abd** (564 AD)
  5. **Al-Harith ibn Hilliza** (~570 AD)
  6. **Salama ibn Jandal** (~600 AD)
  7. **Qays ibn al-Khatim** (~620 AD)
  * ... followed by others such as Amir ibn al-Tufail, Labid ibn Rabi'ah, Mu'awiya I, etc.
