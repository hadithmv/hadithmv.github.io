This image is a full-screen screenshot of **Al-Maktaba Al-Shamela (المكتبة الشاملة)**, a widely used electronic Islamic library software program for Windows.

* **Active Tab:** **"القرآن الكريم"** (*The Holy Quran*).

  3. *Ar-raḥmāni r-raḥīm* (3)
  4. *Māliki yawmi d-dīn* (4)

### **2. Right Sidebar (Navigation & Tafsir)**
* **Tafsir (Explanations) Panel:** A scrollable list of prominent Quranic commentary books linked to the current text, including:
  * *Tafsir Al-Tustari*
  * *Tafsir Al-Tabari (Jami' al-Bayan)*
  * *Zad Al-Masir* (Ibn Al-Jawzi)
  * *Tafsir Ibn Kathir*
  * *Tafsir Ibn Kamal Pasha*
  * *Al-Jadwal fi I'rab Al-Quran*
  * *Al-Tafsir Al-Waseet*
  * *Al-Tafsir Al-Munir* (Al-Zuhayli)
* **Surah Selector (Far Right):** List of chapters starting from `[1] الفاتحة` (Al-Fatiha) down through the surahs of the Quran.
* **Page/Juz Navigation (Bottom Right):** Controls to quickly navigate by **Juz** (Part 1), **Hizb** (1st Hizb), **Rub'** (1st Quarter), and page numbers.

---

### **3. Search Panel (Bottom)**
* **Search Filters (Bottom Right):** Checkboxes for advanced Arabic search parameters, such as:
  * Morphological search (*بحث صرفي*)
  * Affix matching (*بحث باللواحق*)
  * Diacritics matching (*مراعاة التشكيل*)
  * Hamza matching (*مراعاة الهمزات*)
* **Search Results Area (Bottom Left):** A empty table ready to display search results with column headers for *Surah*, *Ayah*, *Page*, *Text*, and *Serial Number*.

---

### **4. Top Bar & Navigation**
* **Application Title (Top Left):** *المكتبة الشاملة* (Al-Maktaba Al-Shamela) logo with export options (PDF) and global search field.
* **Tab Navigation Bar:** Shows multiple open tabs, including search tabs for specific Hadiths/phrases (`...بحث: بَيْنَا أَنَا... صَوْمُهُمْ`, `...بحث: مَنْ بَاعَ`).
