### Key Sections of the Interface:

#### 1. Filter Toolbar (Top)
Allows users to refine search and display criteria:
* **المطبوعات (Printed Publications):** Filter by printed books (**كتب**) or journals (**مجلات**), with options to filter by whether text matches the physical print layout (*يوافق المطبوع / لا يوافق المطبوع*).
* **غير المطبوعات (Non-Printed Materials):** Filter by manuscripts (**مخطوطات**), academic theses (**رسائل جامعية**), digital e-books (**إلكترونية**), or audio transcriptions (**تفريغات**).
