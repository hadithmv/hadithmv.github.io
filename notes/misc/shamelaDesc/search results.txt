This cropped image shows the **Copy with Citation** feature in **Al-Maktaba Al-Shamela**:

---

### **Text & UI Elements:**
* **Tooltip Text:** **`نسخ مع العزو`** (*Copy with Citation / Copy with Attribution*)
* **Keyboard Shortcut:** **`Ctrl+Shift+C`**
* **Highlighted Icon:** The middle icon featuring the stylized Arabic letter **`ش`** highlighted in blue.
* **Top Toolbar Controls:**
  * **`بحث في الصفحة`** (*Search within the current page*) text field.
  * Document and copy action buttons next to it.
* **Background Reading Text:** Shows text from the current page (*... الترغيب والتر... عسقلاني. وعلى مصنف عبد*).

---

### **Functionality:**
Clicking this icon (or pressing **`Ctrl+Shift+C`**) copies the selected text to your clipboard along with its full bibliographic reference automatically formatted at the end—including the **Book Title, Author, Volume, and Page Number**—making it quick and easy to cite sources in academic writing.
